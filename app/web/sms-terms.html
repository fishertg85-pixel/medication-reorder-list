Text Messaging Terms and Conditions

IronLedger Compliance LLC · Effective November 12, 2026

Program

IronLedger Sign-In Codes (NorTex Reorder) is operated by IronLedger Compliance LLC. It sends one-time sign-in codes by text message to employees of pharmacies that use the NorTex Reorder system hosted by IronLedger, so they can sign in securely.

Example message: “IronLedger: Your NorTex Reorder sign-in code is 123456. Do not share this code.”

Who receives messages and how you agree

Only employees whose employer has given them a login. You agree to receive these messages by signing the IronLedger SMS consent form when your login is created. Messages are transactional sign-in codes only — no marketing.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies: a code is sent only when you sign in on a new device or after a period without signing in (typically a few messages per month).

Cost

Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile plan. IronLedger does not charge you for these messages.

Stopping and help

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages; you will receive one confirmation message. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at admin.ironledger@proton.me or (945) 390-2699. After STOP you will not receive sign-in codes, so you will not be able to sign in until your account manager arranges another method; to start again, reply START or contact us.

Delivery

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery depends on your carrier and coverage.

Privacy

See our Privacy Policy: ironledgerrx.com/privacy.html. We never sell or share your mobile number for marketing.
