Privacy Policy — Text Messaging

IronLedger Compliance LLC · Effective November 12, 2026

This policy explains how IronLedger Compliance LLC ("IronLedger", "we") handles information for the IronLedger Sign-In Codes (NorTex Reorder) text messaging program. The program sends one-time sign-in codes to employees of pharmacies that use the NorTex Reorder system hosted by IronLedger.

Information we collect

The employee's name, login ID, mobile phone number, the pharmacy or pharmacies they work for, and records of sign-ins and of the codes we send (date, time, delivery status). The mobile number is provided by the employee, with their signed consent, to their employer's account manager when their login is created.

How we use it

Only to send one-time sign-in codes when the employee signs in, to confirm it is really them, and to keep the system secure. We do not send marketing or promotional messages.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share mobile phone numbers or text messaging consent with anyone for marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

We use service providers only to operate the program — Amazon Web Services delivers the messages and hosts the system — and they may use the information only to provide that service. We may disclose information if required by law.

Keeping it safe and how long we keep it

Information is stored encrypted on Amazon Web Services and is available only to authorized people. Mobile numbers are kept while the employee has an active login and removed or deactivated when the employer switches the login off, unless we must keep records longer by law.

Your choices

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help. Because codes are needed to sign in, stopping texts means you will need your account manager to set up another way to sign in. You may ask us what information we hold about you or ask for it to be corrected by contacting us below.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above shows the latest version.
