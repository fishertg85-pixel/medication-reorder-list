What we do

IronLedger Compliance LLC builds and hosts software that helps pharmacies keep accurate records, reduce errors and save staff time.

Our hosted Reorder List lets pharmacy teams track medications to reorder, see where each item was last ordered from, and keep a permanent archive of submitted orders across one or more locations.

Secure sign-in by text

Pharmacy employees who use our software receive one-time sign-in codes by text message after they sign a consent form with their employer. We never send marketing texts and never sell or share mobile numbers.

Message frequency varies with sign-ins. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.
